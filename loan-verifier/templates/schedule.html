Payment ledger — where each real payment's money went

One row per real payment (not per installment) — each payment's principal and interest are applied to whichever installment is still open at that point, in order. If a payment doesn't fully cover an installment's own remaining need, that installment stays partially open and the NEXT payment finishes it off first before rolling its own excess forward — so a payment can touch more than one installment, and an installment can take more than one payment to clear.
